Arishem Technologies HR-202: Part-Time and Contractor Policy
Effective Date: June 1, 2025

This policy supplements HR-201, which covers full-time employees. Anything not
stated here follows HR-201.

1. Part-Time Employees: A part-time employee is anyone contracted for fewer than 30
hours per week. Part-time employees receive 10 days of paid time off per calendar
year, pro-rated to their contracted hours. Unused paid time off for part-time
employees is not carried over to the following year.

2. Part-Time Sick Leave: Part-time employees receive 5 days of paid sick leave per
calendar year.

3. Contractors: Contractors are engaged through an agency and are not employees.
They are not eligible for paid time off, sick leave or parental leave under HR-201.
Any time off is arranged directly between the contractor and their agency.

4. Contractor Onboarding: Contractors must complete the security awareness training
within 7 days of their start date. Their system access is suspended automatically if
the training is not completed in time.

5. Conversion to Full-Time: A contractor may be converted to a full-time employee
after at least 6 months of continuous engagement, with the approval of a director in
the hiring department. On conversion, HR-201 applies from the conversion date.

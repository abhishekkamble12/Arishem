Arishem Technologies FIN-501: Travel and Expense Policy
Effective Date: January 15, 2026

1. Meals: Meal expenses are reimbursed up to $50 per day for domestic travel and up
to $75 per day for international travel. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

2. Receipts: An itemised receipt is required for any single expense over $25.

3. Submission: Expenses must be submitted through Expensify within 30 days of the
date the expense was incurred. Expenses submitted later than that are not reimbursed.

4. Approval Limits: A manager can approve expenses up to $500. Expenses from $500 up
to $5,000 require a director's approval. Anything above $5,000 requires approval from
the CFO.

5. Flights: Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium
economy may be booked for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class is not
reimbursable.

6. Reimbursement: Approved expenses are paid with the next payroll run, and no later
than 10 business days after approval.

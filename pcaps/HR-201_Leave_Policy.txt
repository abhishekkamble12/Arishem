Arishem Technologies HR-201: Employee Leave Policy
Effective Date: March 15, 2025

1. Annual Leave (PTO): All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid time off per calendar year. Up to 5 days can be carried over to the next year.
2. Sick Leave: Employees are provided 10 days of paid sick leave annually. A doctor's note is required for consecutive sick leaves exceeding 3 days.
3. Parental Leave: Arishem offers 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave for the birth or adoption of a child.
4. Bereavement Leave: Up to 3 days of paid leave is granted in the event of the death of an immediate family member.
5. Approval Process: PTO must be requested via the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by the direct manager.

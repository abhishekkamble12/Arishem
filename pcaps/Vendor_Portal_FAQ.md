# Vendor Portal FAQ

This page answers common questions from suppliers who invoice Arishem Technologies.

## How do vendors log in?

Vendors sign in at vendor.arishem.com with the credentials sent by the procurement
team during onboarding.

## What are the payment terms?

Invoices are paid on net-45 terms: payment is due 45 days after Arishem receives a
valid invoice. Invoices must quote a purchase order number or they are returned.

## Who do vendors contact with questions?

Email procurement@arishem.com. The procurement team replies within two business days.

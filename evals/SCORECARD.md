## Reading these numbers

- **Faithfulness is saturated.** Every answered question scored 1.0. That is credible only because the same judge was shown to score fabricated answers 0.0 (`evals/judge_control.py`), but a ceiling score means this set is too easy to discriminate: these are single-fact lookups over a corpus small enough that every query retrieves all of it. It shows the system does not hallucinate on easy questions, and nothing about hard ones.
- **Answer relevancy is mostly measuring length here.** Its correlation with answer word count is +0.71: answers of four words or fewer average 0.289, longer ones 0.877. RAGAS scores relevancy by regenerating the question from the answer, which a correct one-word answer cannot do. Read it as a verbosity signal, not a quality one.

## Caveats

- Small golden set; treat differences under ~0.05 as noise.
- The judge (qwen/qwen3.8-27b) is a different model family from the generator (openai/gpt-oss-20b), so it is not grading its own output.
- The sweep's best threshold is fitted on this set. With few negative examples it is a starting point, not a calibration; add unanswerable questions before trusting a precise value.
